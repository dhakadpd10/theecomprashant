# Third-party work included

- `skills/` (ig-audit, ig-caption, ig-carousel, ig-comment, ig-dm, ig-human, ig-plan, ig-profile, ig-reel, ig-reply, ig-repurpose, ig-story, ig-viral): instagram-agent-skill by Jake Schincariol, MIT licence, see `skills/LICENSE-instagram-agent-skill`. The hook scorer, caption linter and human-sound checker are used by the weekly planner.
- Voice: Kokoro-82M (Apache-2.0) through kokoro-js. Speech check: Whisper (MIT) through Transformers.js.
- Fonts: Archivo Black, Inter, Noto Sans, Noto Sans Symbols 2 (SIL Open Font Licence) through Fontsource.
- Motion reels: rendered with HyperFrames by HeyGen (Apache-2.0, npm `hyperframes`) and GSAP (GreenSock standard licence, free). The craft rules in `docs/MOTION.md` are distilled from iart.ai's open-source motion skills (https://github.com/iart-ai/motion-skills, MIT). FFprobe through ffprobe-static.
