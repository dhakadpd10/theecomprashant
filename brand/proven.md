# Proven ideas bank (@theprashant.ai)

Prashant's rule (13 Nov 2026): **we don't invent ideas, we remake ones that already hit big**, in our clay style, in our own words, for an Indian audience. Every post the planner makes names one entry below (`"proven": "P07"`). Remake the idea and the structure; never copy another creator's words, images or list order. Instagram labels copies "unoriginal" and stops showing them to new people.

Numbers are likes (L), comments (C) or views (V) found on public posts between Oct and Nov 2026. A bigger number means a safer bet.

## How to remake an entry (every post)

1. **Same promise, sharper for India.** Keep the proven hook's shape ("N websites that feel illegal to know") and make it specific: who, what they get, rupees where it fits.
2. **Every item real and checked.** Sites, tools and prompts must exist and work today. If unsure, leave it out.
3. **More value than the original.** One extra useful line per item (who it's for, the catch, the free limit).
4. **Clay style** (docs/CLAY.md). The look is ours; the idea is proven.
5. **The send line and the keyword** at the end, as in docs/MONEY.md.

Never use the same entry twice in 7 days. Carousels use P01 to P06; reels use P07 to P12.
