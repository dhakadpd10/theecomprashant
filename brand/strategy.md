# Strategy for the week of 2026-10-29

## Prashant's standing decisions (these outrank everything below)

The page is about real ways to make money with AI, for a broad Indian audience (students, 9-to-5ers, freelancers, homemakers, small business owners). The playbook is docs/MONEY.md. Apply it to every reel:
- money in the first line and the first second (a rupee figure, "paid", a named real website, company or person)

## Evidence so far

What this means: Instagram shows reels to non-followers mostly on watch time and sends per reach. With zero sends we never left the test batch. Seller tips were useful but nobody had a friend to send them to. The new niche is chosen for sends.

## What to judge in the first week of the new niche
- Sends per reach on every reel (target: any sends at all, then above 1%).
- Which kind gets the most sends and saves: sites, prompt, breakdown, news, skills or skit.
- Which keyword gets the most comments.
- Keep the kind that wins at least 2 of the 3 numbers and give it more slots next week.
