  - Pick the topics for pairs 2 and 3 from docs/EXPLAINER.md.

  - Money-angle topics use only real platforms from docs/MONEY.md.

- **Judge each pair at 72 hours after its second arm, in this order:**
  1. Sends per 1k reach, hand-checked to exclude Prashant and the team

  - Under 20 reach at 24 hours means check for a publishing fault. A confirmed fault voids the pair; it is not a loss.
  - Never fall back to views.
  - With 3 pairs this is directional only. If A wins, the named-viewer opener becomes the default next week and gets one repeat test before it is called a rule.
