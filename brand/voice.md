# Voice: Make Money with AI page (from 28 Oct 2026)

**Who's talking:** Prashant, an Indian founder who builds AI systems for brands (Spexy Studio) and has run AI on his own store. He earns with AI himself, so he talks like the older friend who already figured it out: practical, not a guru. Shows the tool, says what it pays, admits the catch.

**Who's listening:** anyone in India who wants a side income with AI: college students, 9-to-5ers, freelancers, homemakers, people between jobs, small shop owners. Mostly beginners on a phone, with no money to invest and little time. They have one friend in mind for every money tip, and that's who they send it to.

**How it sounds:**
- Short sentences. Plain words. Numbers over adjectives (₹, hours, days, counts).
- Rupees for money. Dollars only when a site pays in dollars, and then say so.
- Indian context where it fits: UPI, hostel, salary day, Fiverr in INR, WhatsApp groups, Internshala.
- Every post gives away something real that works today, free.
- Always has "The catch": one honest limit (it takes weeks, not days; pay varies; not every country is accepted).
- Caption formula: Comment KEYWORD for the full prompt → the list or steps → what you need to start → the catch → "Send this to the friend who…" → keyword again → "Results vary." → 3 hashtags.

**Never:**
- Invented results, income, clients or screenshots under Prashant's name. Example numbers are labelled as examples.
- Invented websites, programs or pay rates. Only platforms from docs/MONEY.md or ones this week's research found on the company's own site.
- "Get rich", "passive income while you sleep", "guaranteed", "10x", "game-changer", "unlock", "dive in", em dashes.
- Anything that needs a joining fee, crypto, trading, betting, MLM or "buy my course".
- Using "Claude" as the first word of a spoken line; start with another word.

**Visual identity (from 28 Oct 2026):** editorial motion reels on a warm paper background, hosted by Prashant as a 2D modern-vector character (navy hoodie, glasses) with his pressure cooker that whistles on the money moment. Clean cards for each website, prompt or step. How to write them: docs/MOTION.md ("The editorial theme" and "The host").

**Storytelling first (Corey Haines):** the money outcome first, then proof you can check (a real site, a real prompt, a real price), then the steps, then the catch. One idea per reel.
