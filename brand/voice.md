# Voice: AI for D2C page

**Who's talking:** Prashant, an Indian D2C founder who runs AI on his own brand (MiniKin) and builds AI systems for other brands (Spexy Studio). Practical, not a guru. Shows the tool, says what it does, admits the catch.

**Who's listening:** D2C and Shopify store owners in India (and anywhere), 1–20 person teams, doing ₹1L–₹1Cr a month. They run Meta ads, sell on their own site plus marketplaces, and are short on time and staff.

**How it sounds:**
- Short sentences. Plain words. Numbers over adjectives (₹, %, minutes, counts).
- Rupees for money. Indian context where it fits: COD, UPI, Diwali, WhatsApp, Meesho/Amazon/Flipkart.
- Every post gives away something real that works today, free.
- Always has "The catch": one honest limit.
- Formula from the reels: Comment KEYWORD → READ BELOW numbered steps → What you get → Why it works → The catch → keyword again → 3 hashtags.

**Never:**
- Invented results, revenue, clients or screenshots under Prashant's name. Example numbers are labelled as examples.
- "Get rich", "passive income", "10x", "game-changer", "unlock", "dive in", em dashes.
- Promising automation the free tool doesn't do.
- Using "Claude" as the first word of a spoken line (the Fenrir voice says it as "Claw"); start with another word.

**Visual identity (from 20 Oct 2026):** 3D cartoon story reels, a two-person sitcom. Bunty (a loud, lovable Jaipur kurta-brand founder, pink kurta, moustache) has the problem; Prashant (calm AI guy, navy hoodie, glasses) fixes it with AI on his phone. Bright stockroom set, word-pop captions with yellow key words, a cardboard-sign cover. Voices: Kokoro hm_omega (Bunty) and hm_psi (Prashant). How to write them: docs/STORY.md.

**Storytelling first (Corey Haines):** a story with stakes beats a list. Specific moment, the mistake, the fix shown on screen, a twist, one ask.
