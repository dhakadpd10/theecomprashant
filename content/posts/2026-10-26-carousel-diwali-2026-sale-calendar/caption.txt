Diwali is 8 Nov. Your real deadline is earlier, because orders take days to reach buyers. Here's what to do each day 👇

1. This week: lock your offer and stock. Pick one main offer. Count stock for your top 5 sellers. Reorder now if anything runs out within two weeks.

2. By 1 Nov: creatives done. Ads, banners, WhatsApp messages and email, all approved and scheduled. Meta ad review can take a day, so don't upload on launch day.

3. Set your last dispatch date. Ask your courier for transit days. Example: if it's 4 days, dispatch by 2 Nov for Dhanteras and 4 Nov for Diwali. Show that date on your site.

4. 2 to 8 Nov: the big push. Dhanteras is 6 Nov, Diwali 8 Nov. Once your dispatch date passes, stop promising festival delivery and sell e-gift cards.

5. 9 Nov: clearance goes out. Plan it now. Pick the slow movers, set the discount, write the message. Send it the morning after Diwali.

6. Bhai Dooj: 11 Nov. One source says 10 Nov, so check before you schedule anything. Push only gifts you can still deliver, or digital gift cards.

Amazon and Flipkart Diwali sale dates aren't confirmed yet, so don't plan around them.
