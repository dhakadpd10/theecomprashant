Every Diwali offer comes out of your margin. Here's what 5 of them actually cost you on a ₹999 product 👇

Quick setup. These are example numbers, not a real brand. Say your product sells for ₹999 and costs you ₹450 to make, pack and ship. You keep ₹549 an order before ads.

1. 20% off: you keep ₹349.
The shopper pays ₹799. You've just given away ₹200, over a third of your margin. Of the five, it costs you the most.

2. Flat ₹150 off: you keep ₹399.
Rupees off feel real to a buyer. "₹150 off" lands harder than "15% off" on a ₹999 product. And it's ₹50 cheaper for you than 20% off.

3. Free gift: you keep ₹429.
In this example the gift costs you ₹120 but feels worth ₹299 to the shopper. Your price stays ₹999. The product never looks cheap.

4. Buy 2, save 15%: you keep ₹798 an order.
Two units go for ₹1,698. Each unit only keeps ₹399. But the order keeps ₹798, not ₹549. Both units ship in one box, so you'll likely save on shipping too. That saving isn't counted here.

5. Prepaid only, ₹50 off: you keep ₹499.
It's the smallest discount here. It also pulls buyers away from COD. A COD order that comes back costs you shipping both ways and earns you ₹0.

Bonus: label AI-made models in your ads. One blog says Instagram is cutting reach for unlabelled ones. That's reported, not confirmed. Labelling costs you nothing, though.

The catch: these are my example costs. Plug in yours and the ranking can flip. And AI shoppers aren't real buyers. Use them to spot a weak offer, then test it with real ads.
