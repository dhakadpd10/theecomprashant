The parcel lands. Then most brands go silent. Here are the 3 messages to send after every delivery 👇

(The examples use Glowleaf, a made-up skincare brand.)

1. Day 1: check it arrived, plus one tip. "Hi Riya, your Glowleaf Night Cream arrived today. Tip: a pea-sized drop on damp skin, every night. Anything wrong? Just reply here." If something broke, you hear it in a reply, not in a 1-star review.

2. Day 4: ask for the review in their words. Skip "rate us 5 stars." Ask about the one thing they bought it for: "Has your skin felt less dry in the morning? Tell us in one line." Then add the review link.

3. Around day 21: nudge before it runs out. Time it to when the jar is nearly empty, and change the day to match how long your product lasts. Give a one-tap reorder link. Nothing to refill? Send the item that goes with it.

4. Send all 3 on WhatsApp and use email as backup. The Day 1 check-in counts as a utility message, about 12 paise. The Day 21 nudge counts as marketing, under ₹1 each.
