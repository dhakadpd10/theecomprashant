Here's how the scam works. And the one habit that beats it.

1. AI can copy a voice from the clips people post online. Reels, videos, voice notes. Think of the cousin at the sangeet who does mama so well you'd swear it's him.

2. Then the copy calls you. It's mama's voice. There's been an accident, he's at a hospital, and you need to "send money on UPI now." The panic is the whole trick. They want your money before you've had 10 seconds to think.

3. Hang up. Call back on the number you've already got saved, not the one that just called you. Even if the incoming number looks right, dial it yourself. It takes 2 minutes.

4. Now you decide. If mama picks up and he's fine, your money stays yours. If something really did happen, you'll hear it from him or from someone who's with him.

5. Money already gone? Call 1930 right away. It's the national cybercrime helpline. Then file a report on cybercrime.gov.in.
