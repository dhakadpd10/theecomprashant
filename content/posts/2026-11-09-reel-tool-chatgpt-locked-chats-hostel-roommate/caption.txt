1. Your roommate borrows your phone "just to check one thing", so you delete your ChatGPT chats first. Every time.

2. Lock the chat instead. Think of a locker inside your cupboard.

3. Open settings in ChatGPT and set a 4 digit PIN.

4. Lock the chat you want to keep private. Fingerprint or face works too.

5. Now the cupboard opens, but the locker doesn't. Free users get it too.

What you get: you keep your chats, and only you can open the locked ones, even when someone else is holding your phone.

Why it works: deleting loses the chat for good. A lock keeps it and shuts out everyone but you.

The catch: it's rolling out slowly, so your app may not have it yet. Forgetting the PIN deletes nothing, but resetting it deletes every locked chat, forever. A lock also only keeps people out of the chat. It doesn't change what you typed into it, and that's what the card is for.
