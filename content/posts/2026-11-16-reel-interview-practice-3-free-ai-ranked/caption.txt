3. Paste the prompt into ChatGPT or Gemini Live. Tell it the job you're going for. It plays the interviewer and opens with "Tell me about yourself."

4. Answer out loud, then ask for feedback. Example (not a real candidate): "I'm a hard worker." becomes "In my last job I cut the weekly report from 3 hours to 1."

5. Say the new answer again until it sounds like you, not like a script.

What you get: a first answer you've already said out loud a few times, with one real example in it, before the interview.

Why it works: it's nets before the match. You fumble in practice, where it costs nothing, and the AI points at the vague line so you can swap it for a specific one.
