9-to-5ers, after office: 5 free websites that feel illegal to know. My favourite is the last one 👇

1. remove.bg cuts the background out of any photo in a few seconds. Try it on your LinkedIn photo first. Free at preview size; HD uses paid credits. The free file is fine on screen, not for print.

2. TinyWow merges, compresses and converts PDFs, images and videos in your browser. Use it to put this month's reimbursement bills into one PDF. Free with ads, and free use has limits. Keep salary slips and office files off upload sites.

3. Photopea is a Photoshop-style editor that runs free in your browser and opens PSD files. Open the designer's file and fix one line yourself instead of waiting a day. Free with ads; Premium removes them.

4. Perplexity answers your question and shows the sources it used as numbered links. Good for quick research before a meeting. Free plan, with limited Pro searches on it. Open the sources before any number goes in your deck.

5. NotebookLM (my favourite). Add your PDFs, docs or notes and it answers from them and shows where in the file each answer came from, so you can check it. Free with a Google account, and limits apply. Check your company's rules before uploading work files.

The catch: free tiers change. Check the limit before your work depends on one.
