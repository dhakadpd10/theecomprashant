48 two-word AI commands for students in a hostel or library. My favourite stops the AI guessing 👇

They work in ChatGPT, Gemini and Claude today. Type the two words before your question.

1. Understand it. When the chapter made no sense and class is tomorrow: Explain simply, Real example, Compare both, Use analogy, Key terms, Big picture, Why first, Go deeper.

2. Study it. The week before an exam: Quiz me, Flash cards, Exam-style questions, Mark weak, Revision plan, Memory tricks, Short notes, Viva practice. Paste your syllabus first, or it guesses what's in it.

3. Write it. Before you send an email, SOP or assignment: Shorter please, My voice, Make formal, Hindi please, Fix grammar, Simpler words, Warmer tone, Keep meaning.

4. Organise it. When your notes are a mess: Make table, Step-by-step, Bullet points, Make timeline, Mind map, Group these, Daily plan, Make checklist.

5. Decide it. Choosing a course, a laptop or an internship: Give options, Pros cons, Pick one, Be blunt, Rank these, Worst case, Argue against, Budget first. Add your marks and budget, because it doesn't know your life.

6. Check it. Before you submit or believe an answer: Find mistakes, Ask first, Say unsure, Fact check, Show working, Check maths, Cite sources, What's missing.

My favourite is Ask first. Copy this: "Ask first. Ask me 3 questions before you answer, then wait. My question: [type it here]"

The catch: these are instructions, not buttons. Each app words its answers differently, sources can be made up, so open every link. Your textbook and teacher still come first.
