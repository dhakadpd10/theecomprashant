Your launch page gets one shot with a stranger. These are the 7 questions it has to answer 👇

1. What size is it, really? Real measurements, and a photo in a hand.
2. Who is it for? One line. "For everyone" sounds like it's for no one.
3. Why this price? Show what's inside and what it replaces.
4. When will it arrive? "Arrives by Friday" beats "5 to 7 days".
5. What if it's wrong? Returns in one line, right by the button.
6. Has anyone bought it? No reviews yet, so use testers, pre-orders and your own face.
7. Why buy it now? Launch price, first batch, a gift. Keep it true.
