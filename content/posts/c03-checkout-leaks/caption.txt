About 7 in 10 online carts get abandoned. Most of the leaks are at checkout, after they decided to buy 👇

1. Surprise fees at the last step. Extra costs are the top reason carts get dropped. Put your delivery and COD fees on the product page.
2. No COD or UPI. Plenty of Indian shoppers won't prepay a brand they don't know yet.
3. Forced sign-up. Let them pay with just a phone number.
4. Too many steps. Count your taps from cart to paid. Cut one.
5. No delivery date. "Arrives by Thursday" beats "ships in 3 to 5 days".
6. Nothing says it's safe. You need payment badges, a return line and a real phone number.
7. A slow OTP. I'd test yours on a cheap phone on mobile data tonight.
