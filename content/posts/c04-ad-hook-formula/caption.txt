You get about 3 seconds before someone scrolls past your ad. Hooks that hold people tend to have 4 parts 👇

1. Call out who it's for. "Working mums with school-going kids..."
2. Name the pain in their words. "...tired of dal leaking into the school bag?"
3. Add a number that's true. "We shook this box upside down for 6 hours."
4. Give a reason to keep watching. "Watch what happens at the end."

(That's an example for a made-up lunchbox brand. Swap in your buyer, their pain and your own number.)

The catch: a great hook on a weak offer just gets you cheaper clicks that don't buy.
