Your best ad copy is already written. Your customers wrote it. Here are 5 places it's hiding 👇

1. Your 5-star reviews. Find the line about what changed for them. "My son finally finishes his lunch" is a hook. (example)
2. Support chats. The questions people ask before buying are the doubts your ad should answer first.
3. Return reasons. Each one is a promise your page made by accident.
4. Your rivals' 1-star reviews. What people hate about them is your angle, if you fix it.
5. Instagram comments and DMs. Save the exact words people use when they're excited.

Copy their words as they are. Don't polish them.

The catch: this needs volume. With 20 reviews you'll get a few lines, not a campaign.
