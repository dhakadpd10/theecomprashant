Nobody judges ₹1,299 on its own. If you don't give them a comparison, they'll pick the cheapest one 👇

1. Break it into a daily cost. ₹1,299 for 3 months of use is about ₹14 a day. Less than your chai.
2. Put a bundle next to it. Next to 2 for ₹2,199, your single suddenly looks fair, and some buyers will trade up.
3. Compare it to the real alternative. Not the cheapest copy on Amazon. What would they spend otherwise? A salon visit, a repair, a refill every month.
4. Set a free delivery line. Free over ₹999? Now ₹1,299 is the smart cart.
5. Back it with a guarantee. "15 days. Not happy? Full refund." Often it's the risk that feels expensive, not the price.

(All numbers are examples. Use yours.)

The catch: framing makes a fair price feel fair. It won't save one that's too high.
