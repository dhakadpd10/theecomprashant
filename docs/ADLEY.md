@adley (Adley Kinsman, CEO of @viralish, 1M followers, says 80B views across her portfolio). 97 of her posts were collected with likes, comments and captions; her top reels were read frame by frame.
We copy her system and her technique, never her videos, jokes or words.

## Her rules, the way we apply them

1. **Entertain the masses, educate the interested, sell to the committed.** Her reach comes from relatable entertainment, not lessons. Our version: some of Bunty's reels are pure comedy skits about D2C founder life (no lesson, no phone fix). They bring the reach; the teaching episodes and the free tools convert.
2. **Every caption carries the bio line and a comment keyword**, even on a pure joke: "If you're new here, I'm Prashant..." plus "Comment WORD and I'll DM you...". The entertainment viewer learns who we are and what to get.
3. **Skit shapes that work** (simple, repeatable, scalable):
   - **Contrast:** "Bunty when a customer pays online vs when it's cash on delivery", "Bunty before Diwali vs after Diwali".
   - **Choice game:** someone picks between options and the picks get worse (Bunty lets mummy choose the new product, Prashant lets Bunty pick the ad budget).
   - **Escalation:** the same small thing three times, each bigger, then a twist.
   - **Relatable founder feeling:** the 2 am order notification, the one-star review over a delayed parcel, a customer asking "price?" in the DMs.
   One location, one clear premise you get in a second, no explaining.
4. **Re-post winners.** She posts the same video again weeks later with a new caption. The agent re-posts any reel that did 2x the page's usual views, after 10 days, with a new first line.
5. **Hooks** (her teaching):
   - Every hook makes the viewer FEEL something (shock, embarrassment, envy, relief) and opens a curiosity gap.
   - **The "duh" test:** if someone could answer the hook with "duh", rewrite it.
   - Open on the thing people want (more orders, a calm Diwali, no returns), not on the tool or the product.
   - Create an itch and scratch it only at the end. Design the reel backwards from its payoff.
   - Props help: a sign, a box, a phone, cards.
6. **Views before followers.** Make what people want to watch first; the follow and the comment come from the caption and the next reel.
