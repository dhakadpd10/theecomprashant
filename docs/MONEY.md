# Make money with AI: the playbook (28 Oct 2026)

Prashant moved the page from "AI for D2C sellers" to the broad niche **how to make money using AI**. This file is what the planner and the reel writer follow. It outranks docs/VIRAL.md where they clash.

## Why we changed

Our own numbers (20 reels by 28 Oct): 2,239 views, 9 likes, 8 comments, 3 saves, **0 shares**. Median 121 views. Every reel stalled in Instagram's first test batch.

Instagram ranks reels on three signals: watch time, likes per reach and **sends per reach** (Adam Mosseri, Jan 2025). Sends weigh most for people who don't follow you yet, so a page with 0 sends never reaches strangers. Seller tips (COD returns, product pages) are useful to a few people and sendable to almost nobody. "Here's how to earn ₹X with AI" is something everyone has a friend to send to.

What they share:
1. **Money in the first line.** A rupee or dollar figure, "paid", "earn", "side income". The outcome is the hook, not the tool.
2. **Named, real things.** Websites, apps and prompts with real names you can check. Specifics feel like proof.
3. **A list you can save and send.** "3 websites", "6 skills", "the 3-prompt chain". Something you screenshot or forward to a broke friend.
4. **Borrowed authority or news.** A famous name (Raj Shamani, MrBeast) or a headline ("OpenAI is paying students").
5. **Beginners, students and 9-to-5ers.** No money needed, no coding, from your phone.
6. **Comments often beat likes.** That is the keyword-DM gate doing its job.

## The six reel kinds (config motion.mix)

1. **sites**: "N websites that pay you for AI work." Each one gets a card: name, domain, what you do, what it pays as the site states it, and who it's for. 3 to 5 items, the best one last.
2. **prompt**: "I asked Claude to [make me money from X]. Here's the prompt." Show the prompt being typed and the answer that comes back, step by step. The keyword DMs the full prompt.
3. **breakdown**: one AI side income taken apart in rupees. What you sell, who buys it, what it costs, how to land the first client this week. Realistic numbers, the catch included.
4. **news**: something real from the last 14 days that opens a way to earn (a new program, a new tool, a creator fund, a rule change). Borrowed authority: name the company or the person.
5. **skills**: "N AI skills companies pay for in 2026", or a tier list ("AI side hustles ranked: S to D"). Opinionated and arguable, made for comments and sends.
6. **skit**: a relatable money moment, for reach (salary day vs day 5, "my friend who said AI is a fad", the uncle who asks what you do). Centre stage, punchline last.

## Every reel, every time

- **Second 0:** the money outcome on screen and in the voice. "3 websites that pay you to train AI from your phone." (Any rate you put on screen must come from the site itself.)
- **Sends.** One natural line near the end, aimed at a specific person: "Send this to the friend who's job hunting." Never "share this with 10 friends" or "tag someone".
- **Saves.** The list or steps sit on one clean card the viewer can screenshot.
- **The keyword.** It goes in caption line 1, on the end card ("Full list + prompt: SITES") and once in the last spoken line, after the send line ("Send this to a friend who needs work, and comment sites for the full list."). Never earlier in the reel. Instagram doesn't recommend reels that exist to farm a comment word, but it allows keyword-DM tools, so the reel must be complete and useful without the DM.
- **Length.** 18 to 28 seconds with a new picture every 1 to 1.5 seconds.
- **Language.** English voice. On screen use ₹ for Indian money, and $ only when the site pays in dollars.

## Honesty (non-negotiable)

- Only name platforms from the list below, or ones the weekly research found on the company's own site this week. Never invent a website, a program or a pay rate.
- Pay rates are what the site itself says, framed as "up to" or "the site says", never as a promise. Add "results vary" in the caption.
- Prashant's own earnings are never invented. Example numbers are labelled as examples.
- No "get rich quick", "passive income while you sleep", "guaranteed" or "₹1 lakh in a week". The catch is always said out loud.
- No pay-to-join schemes, crypto, trading, betting, MLM, or "buy my course" ladders.

## Platforms we can name (checked, real)

AI training and testing (pays for your time and judgement):
- **Outlier** (outlier.ai): experts rate and write AI answers; pay depends on skill and country.
- **DataAnnotation** (dataannotation.tech): AI training tasks, mostly for the US and other English-speaking countries.
- **Alignerr** (alignerr.com): AI training by Labelbox, for domain experts.
- **Prolific** (prolific.com): paid research studies, some of them about AI.

Selling AI-made work and services:
- **Fiverr** (fiverr.com) and **Upwork** (upwork.com): gigs like AI video editing, chatbot setup, AI thumbnails, prompt writing.
- **Contra** (contra.com): freelance profiles with no commission.
- **Internshala** (internshala.com): Indian internships and freelance work for students.
- **Topmate** (topmate.io): Indian platform to sell 1:1 calls, guides and templates.
- **Gumroad** (gumroad.com): sell digital products (templates, prompt packs, guides).
- **Canva** (canva.com): become a Canva Creator and sell templates (application-based).
- **Adobe Stock** (stock.adobe.com): accepts generative-AI images if they are labelled as AI.
- **ElevenLabs Voice Library** (elevenlabs.io): share a cloned voice and earn when others use it.

Creator income:
- **YouTube Partner Program** (youtube.com): faceless channels with AI voice and visuals still have to be original to be monetised.
- **Instagram**: brand deals and the reels the page itself makes.

Tools to show doing the work (free tiers): Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, NotebookLM, Perplexity, Canva AI, CapCut, Gamma, ElevenLabs, HeyGen, Kling, Suno.
