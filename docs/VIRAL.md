# What the million-view reels do (research, 21 Oct 2026)

Method: Corey Haines' reverse-engineering framework (skills/marketingskills: social -> reverse-engineering, video -> edit anatomy).
We copy the technique (structure, rhythm, caption treatment, packaging), never anyone's characters, script or footage.

## The rules we take from it

1. **Topic beats format.** Pick a pain almost every small business owner (or anyone selling online) feels this week, solved by something FREE, ideally a named tool people already talk about (Claude, ChatGPT, Gemini, WhatsApp, Instagram, Meta Ads, Shopify, Canva). Narrow topics (RTO rules, GST forms) only when the hook is huge. Test: would a shop owner who has never heard of D2C still stop for it?
2. **Package it as a benefit with a number or a secret**: "This free AI writes your product page in 10 seconds", "3 free Claude tricks that sell for you", "Instagram is hiding this from small brands". The hook text, the cover sign and caption line 1 all say the same promise.
3. **Payoff or shock in the first second.** Open in the middle of the action: the result already on screen, or Bunty's disaster at its peak. Never warm up, never introduce characters. Spoken hook 5 to 9 words.
4. **Short.** 15 to 25 seconds total (40 to 70 spoken words). Every extra second costs completion rate, and completion is what Instagram pushes.
5. **A new picture every 1 to 1.5 seconds.** Cut or punch in on every caption chunk; alternate close-up, two-shot, phone. Whoosh on every cut. Shake on the shock.
6. **Show the proof on the phone.** The fix must be visible (the prompt, the answer, the before and after) for at least 2 shots. Specific beats vague.
7. **Story stakes, even in 20 seconds.** Problem (with feeling) -> the fix shown -> a twist or punchline. Emotion is the engine: shock, embarrassment, relief, smugness.
8. **Series.** Bunty is a recurring character with running jokes (his mummy, his chai, his 40 returned parcels, his rival Pinky's brand). When a topic has more to give, end on a cliffhanger and label the next one "Part 2".
9. **The comment gate early.** "Comment WORD" is caption line 1 and appears on screen in the first half, not only at the end. One easy word, the tool is free, no form.
10. **Volume and testing.** 2 posts a day. Write 3 hooks per reel and keep the strongest; when a reel beats the page median by 3x, make more reels from the same pattern (same pain, new angle) the following week.

## Edit signature (what our engine does)

- Hook title big at the top for the first 2 seconds, keyword-coloured.
- Word-pop captions, 3 to 4 words, white with black outline, yellow key words, centre-low.
- A cut or punch-in on every caption chunk; whoosh on cuts; boing on jokes; record scratch on the twist; music stops under the punchline.
- "COMMENT WORD" sticker appears mid-reel for a beat, and again at the end.
- Cover = Bunty holding a cardboard sign with the promise (3 lines max), face visible.

## What our first 8 reels got wrong (for the record)

6 cartoon reels and 2 story reels: 7 to 116 views, zero likes. Too long (34 to 37 s), slow cuts (every 4 s), narrow D2C topics, the problem told before anything happens, the comment ask only at the end, toy-like visuals.
