# The Ask-Me-First Prompt

Paste everything below the line into Claude (or ChatGPT or Gemini), then type your request where it says, or just type a number from 1 to 5 to use a ready example. Works for any request: a gift, a trip, a CV, a study plan, a reply to a relative, or store work like a product description or a reply to an unhappy customer.

---

You are going to help me with one request. Before you answer, ask me the questions you need answered to do it well. Then wait.

**The one line (I can add this to any request on its own later):**
"Before you answer, ask me up to 3 questions you need to do this well, then wait for my reply."

**Step 1: Ask first.** Read my request. Ask me up to 3 short questions, numbered, about the things that would change your answer the most (budget, who it's for, deadline, what I already have, what I've tried). One line each. If my request is already clear enough, say so and ask nothing. Do not answer yet. Wait for my reply.

**Step 2: Answer.** Once I reply, give the answer in plain words, short sentences and simple steps I can follow today. If I skipped a question, make a sensible choice, say what you assumed in one line, and carry on.

**Step 3: Be honest.**
- Never invent facts, prices, dates, timings, phone numbers, websites, reviews or results.
- Anything that can change or that you can't be sure of (a train time, a hotel price, an exam date, a shop's stock, a delivery date), mark as [CHECK: what to confirm and where].
- In a CV or anything about me, never add experience, marks, skills or jobs I didn't give you. If something is missing, write [ADD: ...] for me to fill in.
- For health, legal or money decisions, give general information only and tell me who to ask.

**Step 4: End with one line:** the single thing I should check or do first.

**Ready examples.** If I type only a number, use that example as my request:

1. **Gift:** Suggest a gift for my sister's birthday, within a budget in ₹.
2. **Trip:** Plan a 3-day trip for me and two friends from my city, cheap and by train if possible.
3. **CV:** Turn my experience into a one-page CV for my first job or internship.
4. **Study plan:** Make a study plan for my exam so I finish the syllabus with time to revise.
5. **Reply to a relative:** Help me write a polite reply to a relative who keeps asking about my salary, marks or marriage at family functions. Kind, firm, not rude.

My request:
[TYPE YOUR REQUEST HERE, OR A NUMBER FROM 1 TO 5]
