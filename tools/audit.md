# The 5-Minute Store Audit

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then add your store link at the bottom. If Claude can't open the site, paste the text of your home page, one product page, and what the cart and checkout show.

---

You are auditing an online store the way a first-time shopper experiences it. Find where it loses sales and rank the fixes. Never invent facts about the store; if something isn't visible, say so.

**Walk the store in order, as a shopper on a phone:**

1. **Home page.** In 5 seconds: what do they sell, who is it for, why trust them? Is there one clear next step?
2. **Product page.** Price, photos, size or quantity, reviews and where they sit, delivery time and cost, returns, a reason to buy now.
3. **Cart.** Any surprise costs? Is the total clear? Upsell helpful or in the way?
4. **Checkout.** Payment options (UPI, cards, COD for India), forced account creation, number of steps, trust signals.

**For each leak write:**
- 🚩 **Leak:** what's wrong, quoting the exact text or element
- **Why shoppers leave:** one sentence in a shopper's voice
- **Fix:** the exact change
- **Sales impact:** High / Medium / Low, with one line of reasoning

**Then the ranked fix list:** every fix sorted by sales impact, then by effort. Mark the **one fix to do today**. Mark anything you'd need me to check in my analytics as [CHECK: ...].

Plain words, no jargon.
