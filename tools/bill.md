# Explain-My-Bill Prompt

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then attach a clear photo of your bill where it says. Before you take the photo, cover your account or consumer number, phone number, address, card or UPI details and any QR code. Works for home bills (electricity, phone, internet, gas, school fee, rent) and shop bills (courier, payment gateway, marketplace, software, shop rent).

---

You are helping me understand one bill. Your job is to explain every charge in plain words, tell me which ones are normal and which to ask about, and check the maths. You are reading a photo, so you may misread numbers. Say so whenever you are not sure.

**Step 1: Privacy check first.** Look at the photo before anything else. If you can see a full account number, consumer number, customer ID, card number, UPI ID, bank details, phone number, home or shop address, or a QR code, stop and tell me which ones. Ask me to cover them and upload again. Never repeat any of these numbers in your answer, even partly.

**Step 2: Read the bill.** Tell me:
- What kind of bill this is and who sent it (the company or school name as printed).
- The billing period, bill date and due date.
- The total amount due, exactly as printed.
- Any part of the photo that is blurry, cut off or too dark to read.

Do not guess. If you can't read something clearly, write [CHECK: what it is and where it is on the bill]. If the photo is too unclear to read the main charges, ask me for a better photo instead of carrying on.

**Step 3: The line-by-line table.** Make one table with a row for every charge, tax, discount, adjustment and previous balance on the bill:

| # | Charge (exactly as printed) | Amount (₹) | What it means in plain words | Normal or Ask? | Read clearly? |

Rules for the table:
- **Normal** means this kind of charge usually appears on this kind of bill (for example, a fixed charge on an electricity bill, or GST on a phone bill). It does not mean the amount is correct.
- **Ask** means I should check it with whoever sent the bill: late fees, penalties, arrears, one-time charges, anything called "other charges" or "miscellaneous" with no detail, add-ons or services I may not have chosen, a charge that appears twice, or a big jump from the last period if the bill shows the last period.
- **Not sure** means you can't tell what the charge is from the bill alone. Say what would help you tell.
- **Read clearly?** is Yes, or [CHECK: ...] if any digit could be wrong (for example, 3 or 8, a missing decimal, a cut-off number).
- Explain each charge from what the bill itself says. Do not quote government tariffs, tax rates, plan prices, fee rules or company policies from memory as facts. If the explanation depends on one of these, write [CHECK: confirm with the provider or their official website].

**Step 4: The maths check.** Using only numbers printed on the bill:
- Add up every line and compare your sum with the total printed. Show your working in one line.
- If the bill shows units and a rate (for example, units used × rate per unit), multiply them and compare.
- If the bill shows a tax rate, check the tax amount against that printed rate only.
- If anything doesn't match, say exactly where and by how much in ₹.
- Then remind me: "I read these numbers from a photo. Check the total and any [CHECK] numbers on the original bill yourself before you pay or complain."

**Step 5: What to ask.** For each line marked Ask or Not sure, write one short, polite question I can send by WhatsApp, email or call. Keep it simple, like something a friend would write. Do not put my account number in the message; tell me to add it myself only when I contact the provider through their official number, app or website. If I ask, write the questions in Hindi or my language too.

**Step 6: Scam check.** Warn me clearly if the bill or any message with it:
- asks me to pay a personal UPI ID or a bank account whose name doesn't match the company,
- threatens disconnection "tonight" or "within hours" and gives a mobile number to call,
- has a short link or an unknown link to pay,
- has a company name, logo or spelling that looks off.

Tell me to pay only through the company's official app, website or office, and to find their contact details myself instead of using the ones in a suspicious message. If nothing looks suspicious, say so in one line.

**Step 7: If this is a shop or business bill** (courier, payment gateway settlement, marketplace commission, software subscription, shop rent), also:
- Split the charges into one-time and recurring.
- For recurring charges, show what they add up to per month, if the bill makes that clear. Otherwise write [CHECK: billing cycle].
- List any GST shown on the invoice as printed. Do not tell me what I can claim back or how to file it; write [CHECK with your CA].
- Flag any per-order or percentage fee, and show one example of what it costs on a single order. Label it clearly as an example, using only the rate printed on the bill.

**Step 8: The short version.** End with three lines:
1. What this bill is for, in one sentence.
2. The total and due date as printed.
3. The one thing to ask about first, and why.

**Rules for the whole answer:**
- Never invent a charge, amount, rate, rule or policy. Mark anything you don't know as [CHECK: ...].
- Plain words. Short sentences. Money in ₹.
- This is not legal, tax or financial advice. If a line needs that, tell me to ask the provider or a CA.
- Never tell me to skip or delay paying. Tell me what to check and who to ask.
- Never repeat private numbers from the photo.
