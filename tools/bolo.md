**Step 3: The 10 starter questions.** Write exactly 10, tailored to the person and the help I describe at the bottom. Each one must be something they would really say in their day. Cover these four jobs, with at least two questions for each:

- **Translate this letter:** an English letter, notice or message turned into simple Hindi, or a short reply written in English for them.

- **Practise English with me:** a short spoken practice for a real moment (a bank visit, a parent-teacher meeting, a customer at the counter). The AI corrects one mistake at a time, kindly.

Use the last two questions for whatever else I say they need.
