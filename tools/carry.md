# The Fresh-Chat Handover

Use this when a long chat starts forgetting what you told it. Paste everything below the line at the end of that same long chat in ChatGPT, Gemini or Claude. It writes a 10-line note. Copy the note, open a new chat, paste the note as your first message, then carry on. Read the note once before you paste it, and fill in anything marked [CHECK: ...].

---

You are writing a handover note for this chat. A new chat will read only this note, so it must hold everything that matters and nothing that isn't true.

**Step 1: Read the whole chat.** Go back to my first message and read every message up to this one, mine and yours. Use only what is written in this chat. Do not add facts from outside it, and do not fill gaps with guesses. If you can't see the start of the chat, or parts of it look cut off, say so in one line before the note.

**Step 2: Pull out what matters.** Find:
- The goal: what I am trying to get done.
- The business: brand name, what I sell, who buys it, where I sell (my own site, Shopify, Instagram, WhatsApp, Amazon, Meesho or anywhere else I mentioned).
- Facts: product names, prices in ₹, sizes, stock, dates, links, supplier or courier names, exactly as I wrote them.
- Decisions we made, and the options we ruled out, with the reason in a few words.
- Style rules I gave: brand voice, language (English, Hindi, Hinglish), length, words to avoid.
- What is finished, and what is still left.

**Step 3: Write the note.** Exactly these 10 lines, with these labels, in this order. Keep each line short, under 40 words. Use plain words.

1. **Goal:**
2. **Business:**
3. **Key facts:**
4. **Numbers:** (prices, quantities, dates, budgets, exactly as stated)
5. **Decided:**
6. **Ruled out:**
7. **Style rules:**
8. **Done so far:**
9. **Still left:**
10. **Next step:** (the single next thing to do in the new chat)

Put this line above the 10 lines, so the new chat knows what it is reading:
"Handover note from my last chat. Treat it as correct unless I change it. Ask me before you assume anything marked [CHECK]."

Show the header line and the 10 lines together in one block I can copy in one go.

**Rules for the note:**
- Never invent a fact, price, number, result, review or promise. If something is unclear or missing, write [CHECK: what I need to confirm].
- If the chat says two different things (a price changed, a plan changed), use the latest one and add [CHECK: earlier it was X, later Y, which is right?].
- If a line has nothing to go in it, write "None yet". Do not make something up to fill it.
- Remove private details: passwords, OTPs, API keys, bank or UPI details, and customers' names, phone numbers or addresses. Write [REMOVED: type of detail] in their place.
- Keep my words for anything I wrote carefully, like a product name, a tagline or a rule.

**Step 4: Check your work.** Under the note:
- List every [CHECK] item as a short question I can answer.
- Give one line on anything important that didn't fit in 10 lines, so I can decide whether to add it.

Write the note now.
