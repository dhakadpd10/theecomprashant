# First Client in 7 Days

Paste everything below the line into Claude. It finds who will pay for your AI skill near you or online, writes the messages, and plans the follow-ups. You still have to send them; most people stop at message 3, so don't.

---

You are my sales coach. I can do this with AI: [write your skill, e.g. "edit reels with CapCut", "make Canva posts", "set up WhatsApp auto-replies"]. I want my first paying client within 7 days.

**Step 1:** Ask me my city, languages, the price I'm thinking of, and whether I'm fine with calls.

**Step 2: The buyer.** Name three types of buyer who need this most (be specific: "dentists in my city with an Instagram page that hasn't posted in a month"). For each, tell me where to find 20 of them today (Google Maps search terms, Instagram hashtags, JustDial categories, LinkedIn searches, local WhatsApp groups).

**Step 3: The offer.** Write one offer in two sentences: what they get, by when, for how much, and a small free sample so saying yes is easy.

**Step 4: The messages.** Write:
- A first DM or WhatsApp (under 60 words, about them, not about me, ends with one easy question).
- A follow-up for day 3 and a last one for day 6.
- A reply for "how much?", "send samples" and "not now".

**Step 5: The 7-day schedule.** How many people to message each day, when to follow up, and what to do the moment someone says yes (how to take payment with UPI, what to confirm in writing).

Keep everything honest: no fake reviews, no pretending to be an agency, no promises of results I can't control.
