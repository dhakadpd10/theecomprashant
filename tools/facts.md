# Store Facts for AI Agents

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then paste your shipping, COD, returns, gift-wrap and festival-cutoff policies (or links to those pages) where it says. Paste the entries it writes into the knowledge base in your Shopify admin. If you're not on Shopify, publish the same entries as an FAQ page on your site. This controls what AI shopping agents are told about your store, not what they finally say, so check their answers now and then.

---

You are helping me write short, correct fact entries about my online store. AI shopping agents (the assistants that answer shoppers' questions and recommend stores) will read these entries. Your only job is accuracy. A wrong fact here is worse than a missing one, because an agent will repeat it to a buyer as if it were true.

**Rules you follow the whole way through:**
- Never invent a fact, number, date, fee, carrier, pincode, timeline or guarantee. If my policies don't say it, write [CHECK: what I need to confirm] instead.
- Never infer one policy from another. "Free shipping" does not mean "free returns". "Exchanges allowed" does not mean "refunds allowed". "COD available" does not mean "COD on every product or pincode".
- Never turn a typical time into a promise. If I wrote "usually ships in 2 days", keep "usually". Don't write "ships in 2 days".
- No marketing words. No "best", "fastest", "hassle-free", "premium". Plain facts only.
- Leave out anything internal: supplier names, margins, fraud rules, blocked pincodes, staff names, customer details. If my text contains any of these, tell me you left them out and why.
- Money in rupees with the ₹ sign. Dates with day, month and year. Times with a time zone (IST unless I say otherwise).

**Step 1: Read my policies.** If I gave you links and you can open them, read every page in full. If you can't open a link, say so and ask me to paste the text. Do not guess what a page says. Then list what you received, one line per source (for example: "Shipping policy page", "Returns section of FAQ", "My notes").

**Step 2: Find the gaps before you write.** Check my text against this list and tell me which ones it doesn't answer. Ask me about the missing ones in one short numbered list, then wait for my reply. If I tell you to skip a question, mark it [CHECK: ...] in the entries.

1. **Shipping:** where you ship (all India, some states, international), dispatch time, delivery time by region, shipping charges, free-shipping threshold, order tracking, what happens if a parcel is late or lost.
2. **COD:** is COD available, on which products, any order value limit, any COD fee, any pincodes or regions where it isn't available (just say "some pincodes" if you don't want to list them), whether you confirm COD orders by call, SMS or WhatsApp before dispatch, and what happens if the buyer doesn't confirm or refuses delivery.
3. **Payments:** UPI, cards, net banking, wallets, EMI, pay-later.
4. **Returns and exchanges:** the window in days and when it starts (order date or delivery date), condition the item must be in, items that can't be returned, who pays for reverse pickup, how to start a return, refund method (original payment, bank transfer, store credit), how refunds work for COD orders, refund time.
5. **Damaged or wrong items:** how many days to report, whether photos or an unboxing video are needed, what you do (replace, refund).
6. **Gift wrap and gifting:** is gift wrap available, its price, gift message, whether the invoice or price is hidden in the parcel, sending to a different address.
7. **Festival cutoffs:** for each festival (Diwali, Rakhi, Holi, Karwa Chauth, Christmas, or others I name), the last order date and time for delivery before the festival, by region if it differs, and whether the cutoff is a promise or a best estimate.
8. **Order changes:** can a buyer cancel or change an order, and until when.
9. **Contact:** how a buyer reaches you (WhatsApp number, email, hours). Only include what I give you.

**Step 3: Flag conflicts.** If two of my sources disagree (for example, the FAQ says a 7-day return window and the policy page says 10 days, example numbers), list each conflict with both quotes and where each came from. Do not pick one. Ask me which is correct. Until I answer, write the entry as [CHECK: 7 or 10 days? FAQ and policy page disagree].

**Step 4: Write the entries.** Once I've answered, write one entry per fact. Each entry has:
- **Title:** the question a shopper would ask, in plain words (for example: "Do you offer Cash on Delivery?").
- **Answer:** 1 to 3 short sentences. Lead with yes or no if the question allows it. Then the number, the condition, the exception. Nothing else.
- **Source:** which of my pages or notes it came from, so I can check it.

Group the entries under these headings: Shipping, COD and payments, Returns and exchanges, Damaged or wrong items, Gift wrap, Festival cutoffs, Order changes, Contact.

> **Title:** Is shipping free?
> **Answer:** Shipping is free on prepaid and COD orders above ₹499. Orders below ₹499 pay ₹60 shipping.

For every festival cutoff entry, add a last line: "Valid for [festival] [year] only." Festival entries go stale, so they must say when they stop being true.
