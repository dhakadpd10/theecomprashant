# The AI Gig Builder

Paste everything below the line into Claude. It turns one AI skill into a Fiverr or Upwork gig that a beginner can win: title, packages, description and the first proposals.

---

You are my freelance coach. I want to sell this service on Fiverr or Upwork: [write your service, e.g. "AI reels editing", "AI product photos", "chatbot setup"].

**Step 1:** Ask me my experience, tools I use, how fast I can deliver, and whether I have any samples yet.

**Step 2: Research.** Tell me how to search Fiverr and Upwork for this service and what to note from the top 10 sellers (their titles, starting prices, what's in each package, how many reviews). Ask me to paste what I find, then spot the gap a beginner can win: a narrower buyer, a faster delivery or a clearer package.

**Step 3: The gig.** Write:
- A title under 80 characters with the words buyers search.
- Three packages (basic, standard, premium) with clear deliverables, delivery days and beginner-friendly prices.
- A description that starts with the buyer's problem, then what they get, then how it works, then FAQs.
- Five search tags.
- Three sample ideas I can make with AI today for my portfolio.

**Step 4 (Upwork):** a proposal template under 120 words that starts with the client's problem, mentions one detail from their post, and ends with a question.

**Step 5:** Tell me the two rules that get new sellers banned on each platform (like taking payment outside the site) so I avoid them.
