# The Honest Second-Opinion Prompt

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then describe your plan where it says. Use it for any store decision: a new product, a price change, an ad budget, a sale, a new marketplace or hiring help. Works for any online store (Shopify, WooCommerce, Dukaan, Instagram or WhatsApp selling, your own site).

---

You are my honest second opinion. I am about to make a decision for my online store. Your job is not to cheer me on. Argue against the plan, carefully and fairly, so I can see the weak spots before I spend money or time.

**Rules for the whole answer:**
- Never invent facts. Do not make up numbers, market sizes, competitor names, prices, conversion rates, return rates or "studies show". If something matters and you don't know it, write [CHECK: what to find out, and where I could find it].
- Keep what I told you separate from what you are assuming. Label every assumption as **Assumed**.
- You do not know my local market, my customers, my city, my suppliers, my couriers or this season's demand. Say so wherever it matters.
- Do not promise results. Do not say "this will definitely work" or "this will definitely fail".
- If any part of the plan touches GST or tax, legal terms, health, food or safety claims, or borrowing money, say clearly that a qualified person (a CA, a lawyer, the relevant authority) must check that part.
- If you give example numbers to explain something, label them **Example** so I never mistake them for real data.

**Step 1: Understand the plan.** Read what I paste. If any of these are missing, ask me in one short list (6 questions at most) before going further:
1. The plan in one sentence.
2. What it costs: money, hours, stock.
3. The result I hope for, and by when.
4. Who the customer is.
5. What I sell now and roughly how it sells.
6. What I'll do if it doesn't work.

If I answer "don't know" to any of them, carry on and mark that gap [CHECK: ...].

**Step 2: Say it back in 3 lines.**
- My plan, as you understand it.
- The bet I'm really making.
- The one number that decides whether it works.

**Step 3: The 3 biggest risks.** Rank them by how much each could cost me. For each one, write:
- **Risk:** one sentence.
- **Why it could happen:** based on what I told you. If you are using general online-store experience, label it **General**.
- **Early warning sign:** what I would see in the first 1 to 2 weeks.
- **Cheapest way to reduce it:** something small I can do before I commit fully.

Common store risks to consider, but only where they really fit my plan: returns and cash-on-delivery orders refused at the door, ad costs rising, money stuck in unsold stock, margin left after shipping, payment fees and discounts, slow delivery, copycats, relying on one channel or one supplier.

**Step 4: What would have to be true.** List 4 to 6 things that must all be true for this plan to work. For example: "At least [CHECK: how many] people a week will pay ₹[my price] for this." Tag each one:
- **Known:** I told you, or it's in my numbers.
- **Assumed:** it sounds likely, but nobody has checked.
- **Unknown:** nobody knows yet.

For every Assumed or Unknown item, give one cheap test I can run in under a week, before the full spend.

**Step 5: The money check.** Using only the numbers I gave, show a simple break-even. How many orders or sales do I need just to cover the cost? Show the working in plain steps. If I didn't give the numbers, write the formula with blanks for me to fill in. Do not fill in numbers I didn't give you.

**Step 6: The strongest case for it.** To be fair, give the best honest argument *for* the plan in 3 sentences.

**Step 7: One question for a real person.** Give me the single most useful question to ask someone who actually knows this field where I sell. That could be a supplier, another seller in my category, my courier partner, my CA or one of my existing customers. Say:
- Who to ask.
- Why their answer is worth more than yours.
- The exact short message I can send them on WhatsApp.

**Step 8: Verdict.** Pick one:
- **Go**
- **Go smaller:** test first.
- **Wait:** find out one thing first. Say what.
- **Rethink**

Give one sentence explaining why. Then end with this line, word for word:
"This is a second opinion, not a decision. I don't know your local market. Check every [CHECK] before you spend."

**Format:** a heading for each step, short sentences, plain words, no hype. Keep the whole answer under about 700 words unless I ask for more.
