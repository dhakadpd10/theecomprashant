# The 12-Customer Trial

Paste everything below the line into Claude. At the bottom, paste your new product: the product page link or text, the price, and what the photos show.

---

You are running a 12-Customer Trial on a product before launch. Your job is to find out honestly whether people would buy it, and what to fix first. Do not be polite. Do not invent facts about the product.

**Step 1: Read the product.** If I gave a link and you can open it, read the whole page. If not, work only from what I pasted. List anything important that is missing (size, ingredients, delivery time, returns) instead of guessing.

**Step 2: The prosecutor.** Argue, as strongly as you can, that this product will flop. Give the three best reasons, using only what is on the page.

**Step 3: The defender.** Argue, as strongly as you can, that it will sell. Give the three best reasons.

**Step 4: Twelve jurors, each alone.** Read both arguments twelve times, once as each of these people. Each juror votes without seeing any other vote.

1. College student on a tight budget
2. Busy mom who shops on her phone
3. Gift buyer who needs it to arrive on time
4. Bargain hunter comparing prices in another tab
5. Review sceptic who reads the 1-star reviews first
6. Gym regular who cares about results
7. Premium buyer who pays more for quality
8. First-time buyer who has never heard of the brand
9. Office worker who buys on a lunch break
10. Late-night scroller who found it through an ad
11. Reseller who wants margin
12. Your rival brand, looking for weaknesses

For each juror write: **Vote** (BUY or SKIP), **Why** (one sentence in their voice), **Would change my vote:** (one thing).

**Step 5: The judge.** Show the vote count. Then give the verdict in one line: launch as is, fix first, or rethink. List the **3 fixes** to make before ordering stock, most important first, each with the exact change to make on the page, in the offer or in the price. Mark anything you'd need me to confirm as [CHECK: ...].

Plain words. No hype.
