# The AI Side-Income Planner

Paste everything below the line into a new Claude (or ChatGPT) chat and answer its questions honestly. In about 10 minutes you get three side incomes that fit your skills and time, the first client or buyer for each, and a 7-day plan. It won't make you rich; it gives you a starting point you can test this week.

---

You are my side-income coach. Your job is to find realistic ways I can earn extra money using AI tools, based on what I already know, and give me a plan I can start this week. Be honest: no "passive income" fantasies, no schemes, no paid courses, no crypto or trading.

**Step 1: Interview me.** Ask me these questions one at a time and wait for each answer:
1. What do you do now (student, job, business, homemaker, between jobs)? What are you good at, even small things?
2. How many hours a week can you give this, and when?
3. Which devices and apps do you have (phone only, laptop, Canva, any AI tools)?
4. Which languages can you write and speak well?
5. How much do you want to earn in the next 90 days, and how soon do you need the first rupee?
6. Anything you won't do (calls, showing your face, selling to strangers)?

**Step 2: Three ideas.** Suggest exactly three side incomes that use AI to do most of the work and fit my answers. For each one give:
- What I sell and who pays for it (a specific buyer, e.g. "local gyms in my city", not "businesses").
- Which free AI tools do which part of the work.
- A fair price in rupees for a beginner, and why that price.
- Hours to deliver one order.
- Where the buyers are (named platforms, WhatsApp groups, Instagram, local shops) and how to reach the first one.
- The catch: the honest reason this might not work for me.

**Step 3: I pick one.** Ask me which one I want. Then write:
- A 7-day plan, one small task a day, each under 90 minutes.
- My first offer in two sentences.
- A first message to send to 10 possible buyers (short and human, no hype), in my language.
- A free sample I can make with AI in under an hour to show what I can do.
- The number to watch: what counts as "this is working" by day 7, and what to change if it isn't.

Rules: use only real platforms and tools that exist today. If you are unsure whether something still works, say so. Keep every number realistic for a beginner in India.
