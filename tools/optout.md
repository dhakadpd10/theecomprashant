# The AI Training Switch-Off Card

Paste everything below the line into Claude (the app or claude.ai), then fill in the three short answers at the bottom: which AI apps you use, on what device, and which plan. Claude walks you through the exact taps to stop your future chats being used for training. It also gives you a one-screen card for you and your team listing what never goes into an AI chat, switch or no switch.

---

You are helping me, an online store owner, turn off AI training on the AI apps I use for work. Then you will make me a short card my team and I follow every day. Be exact, be honest, and never guess.

**Ground rules for you:**
- You cannot see my phone, my account or my settings. Never say a setting is on or off. Only I can check that.
- App menus change often. Menu names, toggle names and plan rules may have moved since you learned them. Mark every tap path and every policy detail as [CHECK: ...] so I confirm it on my screen. If I tell you my screen looks different, ask me to describe exactly what I see. Do not invent a menu.
- Never invent facts about how long a company keeps data, what it trains on, or what the law requires. If you are not sure, write [CHECK: ...] and tell me where to look: the app's own settings page, its privacy policy, or its help centre.
- Plain words. Short lines. No scare tactics, no hype.

**Step 1: Ask me first.** If any of my answers at the bottom are blank, ask before you give any taps:
1. Which apps do I use for work? (ChatGPT, Claude, both, or others.)
2. Phone (Android or iPhone), computer browser, or desktop app?
3. Which plan for each? (Free, paid personal, or a business/team plan.)
4. Does anyone else on my team use their own AI account for store work?

**Step 2: The exact taps.** For each app I use, give numbered taps for my device, one tap per line. Mark each path [CHECK: confirm on your screen]. Start from what you know, and confirm each item as you go:

- **ChatGPT:** Settings > Data Controls > the toggle about improving the model for everyone [CHECK: exact toggle name]. Switch it off.
  - Also explain Temporary Chat: what it is and when to use it [CHECK: whether it is still offered and what it keeps].
  - Say whether business plans are treated differently by default [CHECK: on OpenAI's own business privacy page].
- **Claude:** Settings > Privacy > the toggle about helping improve Claude [CHECK: exact toggle name]. Switch it off.
  - Also explain incognito chats and whether rating a reply (thumbs up or down) can still share that chat [CHECK: on Anthropic's own privacy page].
  - Say whether work and team plans are treated differently by default [CHECK].
- **Any other app I name:** don't guess its menu. Tell me to look for words like "training", "improve the model", "data controls" or "privacy" in its settings. Ask me to tell you what I find.

After each app, ask me to reply "done" or describe what I see. If I have the app on two devices, tell me to check whether the setting follows my account or must be set on each device [CHECK].

**Step 3: What the switch does NOT do.** Tell me plainly, in a short list:
- It only covers chats from now on. Chats already used for training cannot be pulled back.
- Deleting an old chat removes it from my history. It does not undo training that already happened.
- Switching it off does not mean the company stores nothing. Chats may still be kept for some time for safety or legal reasons [CHECK: the current retention period on each company's privacy page].
- It only covers my account. Each team member must switch off their own.
- So the real protection is what I never paste in. The switch is a second lock, not the first.

**Step 4: What a store owner never pastes in.** Give me a list made for an online store:
- Customer names, phone numbers, emails, addresses, order IDs.
- Payment details, UPI IDs, bank statements, card info.
- GST number, PAN, Aadhaar, or any ID document.
- Supplier price sheets, margins, contracts, and unpublished launch plans or ad budgets.
- Login details, API keys, passwords.
- Screenshots of dashboards, WhatsApp chats or invoices that show any of the above.

Then show me how to still get help. Give 3 before-and-after examples of the same request, rewritten safely. Replace real details with placeholders like "Customer A", "Product X", "about ₹500", "a city in North India". Make the examples match my store if I told you what I sell. Label them as examples.

If customer data comes up, add one line: India's data protection law (the DPDP Act) may apply to how I handle customer details [CHECK: with a CA or lawyer; this is not legal advice].

**Step 5: The card.** End with one card short enough to fit on a single phone screenshot:
- **Title:** AI Switch-Off Card, plus today's date.
- **The taps** for each app I use (one line each, still marked [CHECK] until I've confirmed).
- **Never paste:** the 6 groups from Step 4, a few words each.
- **Do this instead:** the placeholder rule in one line.
- **Team rule:** everyone switches off their own account and follows the same never-paste list.
- **Recheck date:** one month from today, because app menus change.
