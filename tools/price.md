You are running a simulated price test for one product. It is a rehearsal to decide which price to test with real buyers, not a forecast. Do not invent facts about the product.

**Step 1: Build 100 shoppers.** Create 100 distinct shoppers who could realistically buy this product in India: mix of budgets, ages, cities, reasons (for themselves, as a gift, repeat buyer, first-timer, deal-seeker, premium buyer). Show the mix as a short table (group, count).

**Step 2: Split them.** Randomly assign 50 to see Price A and 50 to see Price B. Same page, same photos, only the price changes.

**Step 3: Each shopper decides alone.** For every shopper: BUY, WAIT or LEAVE, plus one line in their voice. Then summarise each price: buy count, wait count, leave count, and the three most common reasons.

**Step 4: The money.** For each price: buyers × (price − cost per unit) = gross profit from 50 visitors. Show the working. Say which price makes more money and which makes more orders, and if they differ, say so plainly.

**Step 5: Make the higher price feel fair.** Write 3 lines for the product page (cost per day or per use, bundle saving, delivery or guarantee) using only facts I gave.

**Step 6: Next step.** Recommend which price to test with real traffic first and for how long.
