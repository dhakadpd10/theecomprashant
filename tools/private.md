# The What Not to Type into AI Card

Leave the text blank if you only want the card. It works whichever chatbot you use: ChatGPT, Claude, Gemini or Meta AI on WhatsApp.

---

You are making me a one-page "What Not to Type into AI" card. Then you will help me clean text before I paste it into any chatbot.

**Ground rules for you:**
- Never invent facts about any app, setting, menu name or policy. Apps change their menus often. Wherever a point below has a [CHECK: ...] mark, keep the mark on the card so I confirm it on the company's own help page. If you add anything I have not listed, mark it [CHECK: ...] too.
- Never ask me for an OTP, password, PIN, Aadhaar number, card number or bank details.
- If I paste one of these by mistake, do not repeat it back. Tell me which line it was in. If it is a password or PIN I still use, tell me to change it today.
- Use plain words and short lines. I may be new to AI.

**Step 1: Make the card.** Build it for the person named at the bottom. If I left that line blank, make it for "just me." Keep it short enough to fit in two phone screenshots. Use these sections, in this order.

**1. Never paste these into any chatbot**
- OTPs and UPI PINs. No real bank, app or delivery agent needs a chatbot to see them.
- Passwords and login codes.
- Aadhaar, PAN or passport numbers, or photos of these cards.
- Bank account numbers with IFSC, debit or credit card numbers, CVV and expiry dates.
- Other people's private chats, screenshots, photos or voice notes, unless they have said okay.
- **If the card is for a store, add:**
  - customer names together with their phone numbers or addresses
  - full order exports
  - payment gateway or store API keys and secret keys
  - admin logins
  - supplier invoices that show bank details
- **If the card is for a family, add:**
  - children's school and class details
  - medical reports with names on them

**2. Say it without the number.** Give 3 short swaps, in this style:
- Instead of pasting the failed-payment screenshot, write: "My UPI payment failed but money left my account. What should I do?"
- Write 2 more that fit the person the card is for.

**3. ChatGPT locked chat: what it does and doesn't do**
- **What it does:** it puts the chats you choose behind a PIN, so someone else using your phone can't open them. [CHECK: confirm on help.openai.com that this feature exists in your app and in India, and how to turn it on]
- **What it doesn't do:**
  - It does not delete the chat. The chat still sits in your account. [CHECK: on OpenAI's help page]
  - It does not change whether your chats are used to train the AI. That is a separate setting (see section 4).
  - It does not make the "never paste" list safe to paste.
- **PIN-reset warning:** if you forget the PIN and reset it, your locked chats are deleted for good. They cannot be brought back. [CHECK: confirm on OpenAI's help page] Write the PIN down somewhere offline before you lock anything you need.

**4. Turn off training**
- **ChatGPT:** Settings → Data controls → "Improve the model for everyone." [CHECK: menu name in your app version]
- **Claude:** Settings → Privacy → "Help improve Claude." [CHECK: menu name in your app version]
- **Gemini:** the activity setting in your Google account. [CHECK: the current name and place on Google's Gemini privacy help page]
- Turning training off does not delete what you already sent.
- The company may still keep chats for a set time for safety reasons. [CHECK: how long, on each company's privacy page]

**5. The 10-second check before you paste**
1. Would I be okay posting this in a WhatsApp group of 50 people?
2. Can I describe the problem without the number, name or address?
3. Is this someone else's message? Have they said okay?

**Step 2: The cleaner.** If I pasted text at the bottom, clean it using the rules below. If I left it blank, tell me there are two ways to use the cleaner:
- **Way 1, the safest:** don't paste anything. Tell me what kind of text it is, for example "a customer complaint email," "my salary slip" or "a WhatsApp chat with my supplier." I'll give you a checklist of what to delete by hand.
- **Way 2:** delete everything on the "never paste" list yourself first. Then paste the rest, and I'll hide the remaining personal details.

**Cleaning rules:**
- **Replace personal details with labels:**
  - names → [PERSON 1], [PERSON 2]
  - phone numbers → [PHONE 1]
  - emails → [EMAIL 1]
  - addresses and pin codes → [ADDRESS 1]
  - UPI IDs → [UPI ID 1]
  - order or account numbers → [ORDER 1]
  - GSTIN → [GSTIN 1]
  - dates of birth → [DOB 1]
- The same person or number always gets the same label.
- Keep everything else exactly as written, including amounts, product names and the tone, so the text is still useful.
- **Never print a list that matches labels to real details.** That list would put the private details right back on screen.

**After cleaning, show me:**
1. **What I changed:** counts by type only, never the real values. For example: "3 names, 2 phone numbers, 1 address."
2. **Check these:** anything you were unsure about, quoted from the cleaned text.
3. **The cleaned text,** in one block I can copy.
4. **One line:** "Keep your original. After you get your answer, swap the labels back yourself."

**Say this once, plainly:** this cleaner runs inside a chatbot, so it sees whatever I paste. It lowers the risk when I take the cleaned text to another chat or share it with someone. It is still not a safe place for anything on the "never paste" list.
