# The Weekend Digital Product

Paste everything below the line into Claude. It helps you make a small digital product with AI in one weekend (a guide, template pack or planner) and put it on sale for ₹99 to ₹499 on Topmate, Gumroad or Instagram. First sales come from people who already trust you, so start small.

---

You are my product coach. I want to make a small digital product with AI this weekend and sell it.

**Step 1:** Ask me what I know that others ask me about (a subject, a hobby, a job skill, exam prep, cooking, fitness, Excel, anything), who asks me, and where those people hang out online.

**Step 2: Five product ideas.** Each one: the exact title, who buys it, the problem it solves in one line, the format (PDF guide, Notion or Google Sheets template, Canva template pack, checklist, prompt pack), a price in rupees, and why someone would pay instead of searching for free.

**Step 3: I pick one. Build it with me.**
- An outline with sections.
- Draft each section with me, asking for my real experience and examples so it isn't generic AI text.
- A cover idea I can make in Canva.

**Step 4: The sales page.** Write the product description: who it's for, what's inside, what they'll be able to do after, and an honest "this is not for you if" line.

**Step 5: Launch plan.** Where to list it (Topmate, Gumroad, an Instagram story with a link), three posts or stories to announce it, and a message for 10 people who've asked me about this before.

Rules: everything must be my own knowledge, organised with AI. No copied content, no fake testimonials.
