# The Is-It-Real Check Card

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then fill in the three lines at the end (who the card is for, your language, and the forward if you have one). You get a one-screen card to keep on your phone, plus a polite WhatsApp reply in English and Hindi. It works for your family group, and for your shop's customer group when a fake offer uses your store's name.

---

You are making an Is-It-Real Check Card. It is one screen that helps someone in India check a forwarded photo or video before they send it on. You also write a short, polite WhatsApp reply that asks a group to check before forwarding.

**Rules for everything you write:**
- Never invent facts, numbers, phone numbers, websites, limits or features.
- If you are not sure something is true today, write it as [CHECK: what to verify and where]. Do not guess.
- Never tell me a forward is real or fake unless the checks below clearly show it. If they don't, say "not confirmed" and explain what is still unknown.
- Plain words and short lines. Nothing scary, no blaming the person who forwarded it.
- No hype. Never call any tool "100% accurate" or "foolproof".

**Step 1: Ask before you write.** If I have not filled in the lines at the end, ask me:
1. Who is the card for? For example, my parents, my family group, or my shop's customers.
2. Which language? English, Hindi, or both.
3. Do I have a forward to check now? If yes, ask me to describe it in words or paste its text. Do not pretend you can see a photo or video you were not given.

**Step 2: Make the card.** Use these sections in this order and keep it to one phone screen.

**1. Ask Gemini (30 seconds)**
- Open the Gemini app, upload the photo or video, and type exactly: **"Was this made with Google AI?"**
- Gemini looks for Google's hidden SynthID mark.
- The limits, as Google's support page states them:
  - It only detects content made or edited with Google's own AI tools.
  - Videos must be under 90 seconds and under 100 MB.
- Add one line: [CHECK: Google's support page for "SynthID" or "Was this made with Google AI" in the Gemini app, because limits and country availability can change]. If you don't know the photo size or format limits, add [CHECK: photo limits on the same page]. Do not make them up.

**2. What the answer means**
- **Green (a clear answer):** Gemini says it found Google's mark. The photo or video was made or edited with Google AI. Don't forward it as real.
- **Amber (no clear answer):** Gemini says it did not detect the mark. **"Not detected" is not proof it's real.** Other AI tools don't leave Google's mark, edits or screen recordings can remove it, and long or large videos may not be checked. Do the 3 checks below.

**3. Three quick checks before you forward**
1. **Who posted it first?** Is it a known person, news outlet or official account, or a "forwarded many times" message with no source?
2. **Does a news site or PIB Fact Check mention it?** Search a few words from the message along with "PIB Fact Check" or a trusted news site's name. If nobody credible reports it, treat it as not confirmed.
3. **Does it promise money?** Free gifts, lottery wins, "government scheme" payouts, job offers that ask for a fee, or a link that asks for OTP, UPI PIN or bank details. If yes, don't click, don't pay, don't forward.

**4. If money was lost or someone is being cheated**
- Call **1930** (national cybercrime helpline) as soon as possible.
- Or report at **cybercrime.gov.in**.

**5. For shop owners (include only if the card is for a shop's customers)**
- If a forward uses your store's name for a "giveaway", "lucky draw" or discount link, tell customers which channels are really yours: [CHECK: your real website, Instagram handle and WhatsApp number].
- Say clearly that you never ask for OTP, UPI PIN or advance payment to claim a prize. Write this only if it is true for the shop; otherwise use [CHECK: your actual payment policy].

**Step 3: The WhatsApp reply.** Write it ready to copy and send as-is. It should be warm, take under 20 seconds to read, and never name or blame anyone. Start from these and adjust the tone for my group. Keep the facts exactly the same.

English:
> Hi everyone 🙏 Small request: before we forward a shocking photo or video, or any message that promises money, let's take one minute to check it. A lot is made with AI now. Upload it to the Gemini app and ask "Was this made with Google AI?" "Not detected" doesn't mean it's real, so also check who posted it first and whether a news site or PIB Fact Check mentions it. If someone loses money, call 1930 or report at cybercrime.gov.in. Thank you!
