# The Use-Up Reorder Series

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then fill in your product details where it says. It works for any product that runs out (skincare, food, supplements, pet care, cleaning, baby care) and with whatever you already use to send email or WhatsApp (Shopify Email, Klaviyo, Interakt, WATI, AiSensy or similar). It writes and times the messages. You still set up the sending in your own tool.

---

You are building a 3-message post-purchase series for one product that customers use up. Your job is to estimate when a buyer is likely to run out, then write three messages timed around that day: a how-to-use tip, a check-in, and a reorder reminder. You will write a WhatsApp version and an email version of each.

Keep it to 3 messages. WhatsApp marketing messages cost the brand money for every message sent, so every message has to earn its place. Do not suggest adding more.

**Rules for the whole task**
- Never invent facts. Do not make up ingredients, results, benefits, reviews, discounts, prices, delivery times or guarantees.
- If you need something I haven't told you, write [CHECK: what you need] in its place and keep going.
- Make no medical, health or "guaranteed result" claims, even if the product is skincare, food, a supplement or baby care. Stick to how to use it and what I have told you it does.
- Use no fake urgency ("only 2 left", "offer ends tonight") unless I tell you it's true.
- Plain words. Short sentences. Match my brand voice. No hype.

**Step 1: Get the facts.** Ask me for anything below that I haven't given you. Ask for all of it in one go, not one question at a time. If I say "don't know", mark it [CHECK: ...] and use a sensible assumption that you state clearly.

1. Product name and what it is.
2. Pack size: total amount (ml, g, count of pieces, number of sachets or doses).
3. How it's used: amount per use (for example 2 pumps, 1 scoop, 1 tablet) and how often (per day or per week).
4. Who uses it: one person, a couple, a family, a pet, a baby. Does use change with season or age?
5. Delivery time in days, from order to doorstep, for most of my customers.
6. My reorder link, and whether I have a real reorder offer. If there's no offer, write the message without one.
7. The one tip that most helps people get results or avoid mistakes with this product.
8. Channels: WhatsApp, email or both. Have buyers opted in to WhatsApp marketing messages?
9. Language: English, Hindi, Hinglish or another.
10. My brand voice in a few words, or 2 or 3 lines from my site or captions.
11. Optional: if I know it, the usual number of days between a customer's first and second order. In Shopify, you can find this by looking at repeat customers' order dates.

**Step 2: Estimate the use-up day.** Show your working in simple steps so I can check it.
- Uses per pack = pack size ÷ amount per use.
- Days per pack = uses per pack ÷ uses per day (adjust for the number of users).
- Give 3 numbers: a fast user (uses more, or more people share it), a typical user, and a slow user. Say what you assumed for each.
- Count the days from the **delivery date**, not the order date. COD and slow delivery can add days.
- If I gave you my real days-between-orders figure, compare it with your estimate. If they differ, say so and tell me which one to trust: my real data wins.
- If the product doesn't run out in a predictable way (for example it's a one-time buy or a durable item), tell me plainly that this series is the wrong tool, and stop.

Label the result clearly: **Estimated use-up day: Day X after delivery (range Day Y to Day Z). This is an estimate. Check it against your own repeat-order data.**

**Step 3: Set the send days.** Use these rules, and show the final days in a short table.
- **Message 1, how-to-use tip:** 1 to 2 days after delivery.
- **Message 2, check-in:** around 40 to 60% of the way to the typical use-up day.
- **Message 3, reorder:** typical use-up day minus delivery days minus 2 to 3 days of buffer, so the new pack arrives before the old one runs out.
- If the gaps end up too close together (for example the product lasts only 10 days), say so and suggest merging the tip and the check-in. Still send no more than 3 messages.

**Step 4: Write the series.** For each of the 3 messages, write:

*WhatsApp version*
- Written as a template I can submit for approval. Use {{1}}, {{2}} for variables such as first name and product name, and list what each variable is. Don't start or end the message with a variable.
- Under 550 characters. One clear point per message.
- One button idea (quick reply or link) with its label.
- A short opt-out line, such as "Reply STOP to stop these messages."
- The category Meta will likely put it in (Marketing or Utility), with the note: "Meta decides the category when you submit. A reorder reminder is almost always Marketing."

*Email version*
- Subject line (under 50 characters) and preview text.
- Body: 40 to 120 words, one link or button, and a sign-off in my voice.
- A reminder that the email must include an unsubscribe link.

What each message should do:
- **Message 1 (tip):** help them use it right so it works for them. Sell nothing. No discount.
- **Message 2 (check-in):** ask one simple question they can answer in a tap or a word (for example "How's it going so far?"). Tell them they can reply with a problem. Put no hard sell here.
- **Message 3 (reorder):** say they're likely running low around now, give the reorder link, and mention the offer only if I gave you a real one. Tell them to ignore the message if they still have plenty.

**Step 5: The setup sheet.** Give me a table I can copy into my sending tool, with these columns: Message, Trigger (for example "order delivered"), Wait (days), Channel, Stop if.

Include these stop rules:
- If the customer reorders before Message 3, cancel Message 3.
- If they reply with a complaint or a problem, stop the series and pass them to a human.
- If they reply STOP or unsubscribe, remove them from all three.

Then add a cost line. Don't make up a rate:
**WhatsApp cost = number of buyers × number of WhatsApp marketing messages × your per-message rate. [CHECK: your current rate in your WhatsApp provider's dashboard. Rates change by country and over time.]**

**Step 6: What to check and measure.** End with three short lists:
- **Check before you switch it on:** every [CHECK: ...] and [ADD: ...] item, collected in one place.
- **What to measure after 30 days:** the reorder rate inside the use-up window, replies to Message 2, and opt-outs per message.
- **The catch:** this is an estimate of when people run out, not real data. Some buyers will run out earlier or later. Adjust the send days once you see your own repeat orders.
