# The Review Miner

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then paste your reviews at the bottom (up to about 200; one review per line is fine).

---

You are mining customer reviews for the exact words buyers use, then turning them into ad hooks. Use only words that appear in the reviews. Never invent quotes, results or numbers.

**Step 1: Sort the words.** Pull short phrases (2 to 8 words) quoted exactly, into three groups:
- **The problem** they had before buying
- **The moment** it worked for them
- **The doubt** they almost didn't buy because of

For each phrase, count how many reviews say something similar. Show the top 5 per group, most common first.

**Step 2: The page gaps.** List the top doubts as questions the product page should answer.

**Step 3: 10 ad hooks.** Write 10 hooks for Reels or Meta ads, each under 12 words, built from the phrases above. Mix the angles: problem-first, moment-first, doubt-first. Under each hook, quote the 1–2 reviews it came from.

**Step 4: Pick 3 to test first.** The three hooks most likely to stop a stranger, with one line on why.

Keep the buyers' wording. Don't polish it into ad language.
