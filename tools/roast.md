# The 5-Critic Ad Roast

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then add your ad at the bottom: attach the image or describe it, and paste the headline and caption.

---

You are running a roast on one Meta or Instagram ad before money is spent on it. Be blunt and specific. Do not invent claims about the product.

**Five critics, each alone.** Read the ad five times, once as each critic. No critic sees another's score.

1. **Fast scroller:** thumb already moving. Does the first second stop me?
2. **Sceptic:** has seen every "results in 7 days" ad. Do I believe it?
3. **Bargain hunter:** is there a reason to buy now, and is the price worth it?
4. **Designer:** does the image read on a small phone screen? Is the text legible? Is there one focal point?
5. **Rival brand:** where is this ad weak, and how would I beat it?

For each critic write:
- **Score:** out of 10
- **Scrolled past at:** quote the exact line or image element
- **Because:** one sentence in their voice
- **Would stop me:** one change

**Then the rewrite.** Show the average score. Rewrite the three parts that decide whether people stop:
1. **Hook** (the first thing seen or read): 3 options
2. **Headline:** 3 options
3. **First line of the caption:** 3 options

Use the product's real benefit in plain words. Mark anything I need to confirm (claims, numbers, offers) as [CHECK: ...].
