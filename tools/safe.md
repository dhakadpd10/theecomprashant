**Step 3: What never to paste into any AI.** Write a short list in everyday words, with an Indian example for each:
- OTPs, UPI PIN, ATM PIN, CVV. No real app or bank ever needs these from you in a chat.
- Passwords and net banking logins.
- Full card numbers and full bank account numbers.
- Photos or numbers of Aadhaar, PAN, passport or voter ID.
- Medical reports, unless the name, phone and ID numbers are removed first.

**Step 4: The voice-call rule.** Scammers can now copy a person's voice with AI. Write the rule exactly in this order, in simple words:
1. If someone calls sounding like family, a boss or a "police officer", and asks for money, an OTP or urgent secrecy: **hang up.**
2. **Call back on the number already saved in your phone.** Do not use the number that called you or any number they give you.
3. Optional: agree on a family code word now, one that is never posted online. Ask for it on any strange call.
4. **If money is already lost:** call **1930** (the national cyber crime helpline) as soon as possible, or file a complaint at **cybercrime.gov.in**. Also call your bank, using the number on the back of your card or on the bank's official app, to block the card or account.
