# Websites That Pay for AI Work

Real websites where people earn with AI skills, plus a prompt that tells you which ones fit you and how to get accepted. Pay depends on your skills, your country and the work available; check each site's own page before you sign up, and never pay a fee to join anything.

**AI training and testing (they pay for your time and judgement):** Outlier (outlier.ai), DataAnnotation (dataannotation.tech), Alignerr (alignerr.com), Prolific (prolific.com).
**Selling AI-made work:** Fiverr (fiverr.com), Upwork (upwork.com), Contra (contra.com), Internshala (internshala.com) for students, Topmate (topmate.io) and Gumroad (gumroad.com) for guides and templates.
**Selling AI-made assets:** Adobe Stock (labelled AI images), Canva Creators (templates, application-based), ElevenLabs Voice Library (your cloned voice).

- What the work actually is, day to day.
- Who they accept, and whether people from my country can join (say if you're not sure and tell me where on the site to check).
- How they pay out, and roughly what the site says about pay (as "the site says", never a promise).
- The sign-up steps, including any test or assessment, and how to prepare for it.
- One mistake that gets people rejected or banned.
