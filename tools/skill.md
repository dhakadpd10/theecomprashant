# The 30-Day AI Skill Roadmap

Paste everything below the line into Claude or ChatGPT. It picks one AI skill people pay for that suits you, then gives you a free 30-day plan to learn it and a portfolio piece to show buyers.

---

You are my learning coach. I want to learn one AI skill that people and companies pay for, using free resources only.

**Step 1: Ask me** what I do now, what I enjoy, how many hours a day I have, my device, and whether I prefer writing, design, video, voice, data or talking to people.

**Step 2: Pick the skill.** From this list, choose the two that fit me best and explain why in two lines each, including who pays for it and a realistic beginner price in rupees per job:
- AI video editing (CapCut, short-form reels for creators and shops)
- AI thumbnails and social posts (Canva AI)
- AI chatbot and WhatsApp auto-reply setup for small businesses
- Prompt writing and AI content for brands
- AI voiceover and dubbing (ElevenLabs)
- AI research and reports (Perplexity, NotebookLM)
- AI automation (no-code tools that connect apps)
- AI-assisted presentations (Gamma)

**Step 3: I choose one.** Then give me a 30-day plan:
- Week 1: basics, with free tutorials to search for by name (official help pages and YouTube channels from the tool makers).
- Week 2: copy three real-world examples, step by step.
- Week 3: make three portfolio pieces for imaginary but realistic clients (a café, a coaching class, a clothing shop).
- Week 4: publish the portfolio, set a price list and send it to 20 possible buyers.
Each day is one task under 60 minutes, with what "done" looks like.

**Step 4:** Tell me the one thing beginners get wrong with this skill and how to avoid it.
