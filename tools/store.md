# The 7-Shopper Test

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then paste your product page link or its text where it says. Works with any online store (Shopify, WooCommerce, Dukaan, your own site).

---

You are running a 7-Shopper Test on one product page. Your job is to find out why visitors leave without buying, then fix the page.

**Step 1: Read the page.** If I gave you a link and you can open it, read the whole product page. If you can't open it, ask me to paste the page text (title, price, images described in words, description, reviews, shipping and returns info). Do not guess what is on the page.

**Step 2: Seven shoppers, each alone.** Read the page seven times, each time as one of these people. Each shopper answers on their own and never sees the others' answers, so nobody goes easy.

1. **First-timer:** never heard of this brand. Do I trust it in the first five seconds?
2. **Bargain hunter:** is this worth the price? Where is the offer, the comparison, the reason to buy now?
3. **Gift buyer:** buying for someone else. Delivery date, gift wrap, will it suit them, can they return it?
4. **Review sceptic:** reads every review and looks for the bad ones. Are the reviews real, recent and specific?
5. **Midnight scroller:** on a phone at 1am, half-distracted. Can I understand and buy this with one thumb?
6. **Loyal fan:** bought from you before. Is there anything here for me? Would I tell a friend?
7. **Your competitor:** reads the page looking for weaknesses to beat in their own ads.

For each shopper, write:
- **Leaves at:** the exact spot on the page where they would stop (quote it).
- **Because:** one sentence, in their own voice.
- **Would buy if:** the one change that keeps them.
- **Score:** 1 to 10, how likely they are to buy.

**Step 3: The verdict.** Show a table of all seven scores. Then list the problems that more than one shopper hit, ordered by how many sales each one likely costs.

**Step 4: Fix it.** Rewrite the page:
- A new headline and the first two lines under it.
- The questions shoppers asked that the page never answered, with answers (mark anything you don't know as [ADD: ...] for me to fill in; never invent facts, prices, reviews or guarantees).
- A short fix list I can do today, most important first.

Keep the brand's voice. Plain words. No hype.
