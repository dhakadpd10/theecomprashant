# The UGC Script Pack

Paste everything below the line into Claude, then paste your product page link or text, plus 5 to 20 real customer reviews if you have them, where it says at the bottom. Works for any D2C product sold on your own site, Shopify, Amazon, Flipkart or Meesho. You get 30 scripts and a shot list. You still film or render the videos yourself.

---

You are writing a UGC Script Pack for one product. Your job is to turn my product page and my real customer reviews into 30 short, filmable, UGC-style video scripts for Instagram Reels and Meta ads. You never invent facts.

**Step 1: Read what I gave you.** If I gave you a link and you can open it, read the whole product page. If you can't open it, ask me to paste the page text. Do not guess what is on the page. Then check whether you have each of these. Ask me for anything missing in one short list, and wait for my answer before writing scripts:

- Product name, price, and any current offer
- What is in the box (every item)
- Who usually buys it, and whether people buy it as a gift
- Delivery time to metro cities and to smaller towns
- Whether Cash on Delivery is available, and any COD fee
- Return or exchange policy, in my exact words
- Language for the scripts: English, Hinglish or Hindi
- Product stills I already have (list them, or describe them in words)

If I say "skip", carry on and mark every gap as [CHECK: ...].

**Step 2: Build the fact sheet.** Before writing anything, list every fact you will use, each with its source (page, review, or my answer). Then list the unknowns as [CHECK: ...]. Scripts may only use facts from this sheet. If a script needs something that isn't on the sheet, write [CHECK: ...] in the script instead of filling it in.

**Step 3: Pull buyer words.** From the reviews, pull up to 10 short phrases customers actually used: the problem they had, what surprised them, who they bought it for. Quote them exactly. If I gave no reviews, say so and write from the page only. Never write a review yourself, and never present a line as something a customer said unless it is a real quote.

**Step 4: Write 30 scripts, 6 per angle.**

1. **Gift:** buying it for someone else. Who it suits, how it arrives, the moment they open it.
2. **Problem:** the everyday problem it fixes, shown before and after.
3. **Unboxing:** what arrives, the first look, the first use.
4. **Comparison:** this product against the old way of doing it, or a generic alternative ("a regular steel bottle", "the ₹199 one from the market"). Never name or show a competitor brand. No "best", "No.1" or "cheapest" unless my page proves it.
5. **COD trust:** for the buyer who doesn't trust new brands online. Show the real ordering, delivery and payment steps. Only promise COD, returns or delivery times that are on the fact sheet. If COD is not confirmed, make this angle "first order trust" instead and flag it as [CHECK: COD].

Write every script in this exact shape:

**[Angle] #[number]: [short name]**
- **Hook (0 to 2 sec):** words on screen and spoken, 8 words or fewer.
- **Shot 1:** what the camera sees, and what is said or shown as text.
- **Shot 2:** same.
- **Shot 3:** same, ending on the product.
- **Line to camera:** 1 or 2 sentences, said the way a real person talks, not like an ad.
- **Caption:** under 150 characters, ends with one clear next step (link in bio, shop now, or DM).
- **Needs:** props, location, and whether it needs a person on camera or can be made from stills.

Rules for every script:
- 15 to 30 seconds. Vertical 9:16. Filmable on a phone in a home, shop or office, no studio.
- Within each angle, use 6 different hooks. No two scripts open the same way.
- Plain words. No "game-changer", "life-changing", "must-have", "unlock", "10x". No em dashes.
- No health, safety, medical or "results guaranteed" claims unless the exact claim is on my page, and even then mark it [CHECK: proof for this claim].
- Prices and offers only as written on the fact sheet. If an offer might end, add [CHECK: offer still live].
- If you use an example number (for example "saves 10 minutes"), label it [EXAMPLE] so I can confirm or cut it.

**Step 5: Who should say these lines.** Add one short note at the end of the scripts:
- UGC scripts are for real customers or creators who have actually used the product. Don't hand a script to someone who hasn't, and don't present a scripted line as an unprompted review.
- If a creator is paid or got the product free, the video needs a clear disclosure such as #ad or "Paid partnership" (ASCI guidelines in India, and Meta's branded content tool).
- Ask permission before using a customer's real name, face or review in an ad.

**Step 6: Shot list for Meta's image-to-video tool.** Meta Ads Manager can turn product stills into short video ads (the feature name and limits change, so check what your account shows). Give me a table with these columns:

| Still | What it must show | Use in scripts | Motion to ask for | Have it? |

- List 6 to 10 stills: for example a front shot on plain background, the product in hand for scale, in-use, every item in the box, packaging closed, packaging open, a detail close-up, and a text card with price or offer.
- In "Use in scripts", name which script numbers each still can make without filming a person.
- In "Have it?", mark Yes if it matches a still I listed, or [CHECK: shoot this] if I don't have it.
- Then list which scripts can be made from stills alone, and which need a real person on camera.

**Step 7: Film these first.** Pick the 5 scripts I should make first. For each, give one sentence on why, based on my reviews and fact sheet, not on guesses about performance. Then give a simple test: run them as separate ads with the same budget for the same days, and compare cost per purchase (or cost per click if there are too few purchases to compare).
