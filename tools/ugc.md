# UGC Script Pack

Paste everything below the line into Claude. Then, where it says at the bottom, paste one product page (link or text) and up to 5 real customer reviews. Works for any product on any store: Shopify, WooCommerce, Dukaan, Amazon, Meesho, Flipkart or your own site. You get scripts only. You still film, edit and post them yourself.

---

You are writing a UGC Script Pack for one product. UGC means short videos that look like a real person filmed them on a phone, not like an ad agency made them. Your job is to write 30 scripts I can shoot this week with a phone, a window and no crew.

**Rules that apply to every step:**
- Use only facts from the product page and reviews I give you. Do not invent features, ingredients, materials, sizes, prices, offers, delivery times, results, awards, customer counts or guarantees.
- If a script needs a fact you don't have, write it as [CHECK: what I need to confirm]. Don't fill the gap with a guess.
- No health, medical, weight-loss, safety or "guaranteed result" claims unless the page states them word for word. Even then, mark them [CHECK: can we prove this?].
- Never name or mock a competitor. "Other kurtas I've bought" is fine. A brand name is not.
- Plain words. No hype. Don't use "game-changer", "obsessed", "holy grail", "must-have", "10x" or "life-changing".

**Step 1: Read the product page.** If I gave you a link and you can open it, read the whole page. If you can't open it, stop and ask me to paste the page text: title, price, description, sizes or variants, what's in the box, shipping, COD, returns, and the images described in words. Do not guess what's on the page.

**Step 2: Build a fact sheet.** Before writing any scripts, show me:
- **What it is:** one plain sentence.
- **Who it's for:** based only on the page and reviews.
- **Facts I can say on camera:** a bullet list, each with where it came from (page or review number).
- **What buyers actually said:** the best short phrases from the reviews, quoted exactly. Note which review each one came from.
- **Complaints or doubts in the reviews:** quoted exactly. These become objection scripts, so don't hide them.
- **Gaps:** things a shopper would want to know that neither the page nor the reviews answer, each as [CHECK: ...].

If I gave you no reviews, say so. Then write the "Buyer's words" scripts as templates for a real buyer to fill in with their own words, and mark them [NEEDS A REAL BUYER].

**Step 3: Six angles, five scripts each.** Write 30 scripts across these six angles. If an angle doesn't fit this product, replace it with one that does and tell me why.

1. **The problem:** the moment before someone had this product. What was annoying, slow, ugly or uncomfortable?
2. **Buyer's words:** built on real review quotes. The buyer's actual phrases, not polished ones.
3. **The doubt:** the objections a shopper has (price, quality, size, "will it look like the photo", COD, returns, delivery time), answered honestly.
4. **Behind the scenes:** how it's made, packed or chosen. Founder or staff only, and only with facts from the page or marked [CHECK: ...].
5. **First use:** unboxing, how to use it, what it looks or feels like on day one.
6. **The occasion:** gifting, festivals (Diwali, Rakhi, weddings, Eid, Christmas), seasons, or a specific daily moment. Use only occasions that fit the product.

**Step 4: Write each script in this exact format.** Keep each one 15 to 30 seconds when spoken.

**#[number]. [Angle]: [short working title]**
- **Who says it:** Founder, Staff or Buyer, plus one line on why that person fits.
- **Hook line (first 2 seconds):** the first words spoken or shown on screen. Open on a specific moment, a number or a real quote, never on "Hi guys" or the brand name. Any example numbers must be labelled as examples, like "(example: 3 washes)".
- **Shot 1:** what the camera sees and what's said or shown, in one or two lines.
- **Shot 2:** same.
- **Shot 3:** same.
- **Line to camera:** one sentence the person says while looking into the lens. It should sound like talking, not reading.
- **Caption:** 1 to 3 short lines for Instagram or Reels. End with a plain question or next step. No fake urgency like "only 2 left" unless the page says so.
- **Checks:** any [CHECK: ...] items in this script, or "None".

Shots must be doable with one phone, at home, in a shop or in a stockroom: hands, close-ups, a mirror, a doorstep, a window for light. No drones, models, studios or props I'd have to buy.

**Who says it: honesty rules.**
- **Founder:** speaks as the founder. Can talk about why the product exists and how it's made.
- **Staff:** must say or show that they work at the brand ("I pack these every day"). Never written to sound like a customer.
- **Buyer:** only for a real customer, using their own experience and the words from their review. Add this note under every Buyer script: *"Only shoot with a real buyer who agrees to be filmed. If you gave them the product free, paid them or gave a discount, the post must say so clearly (for example #ad or 'gifted'), as per ASCI rules in India."*
- Never write a script where staff, family, friends or an actor pretends to be a customer.

**Step 5: The shoot plan.** After all 30 scripts, give me:
- **A table:** script number, angle, who says it, location, and the number of [CHECK] items.
- **Shoot these 5 first:** the 5 scripts with the strongest hooks and no [CHECK] items, and one line each on why.
- **Batch list:** group the scripts by person and location, so I can shoot several in one go (example: "Founder at the packing table: #4, #11, #19, #22").
- **All the CHECKs in one list:** every [CHECK: ...] from the whole pack, with no duplicates, so I can answer them in one sitting.

**What this pack does not do:** it doesn't film, edit, add music, post or schedule anything, and it can't tell you which script will perform best. Shoot a few, post them, and compare views and comments yourself.
