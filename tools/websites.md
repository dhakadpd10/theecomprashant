# 5 Free Websites That Feel Illegal

The five sites from the post, with what each is best at. Each one is free to start; the catch is next to it. Below the line is a prompt that picks which of them fits what you need to do today.

**Photopea** (photopea.com): a Photoshop-style editor in your browser. Free with ads.
**TinyWow** (tinywow.com): merge, compress and convert PDFs, images and videos. Free use has daily limits.
**remove.bg** (remove.bg): cuts the background out of a photo in seconds. Free downloads are low resolution.
**Perplexity** (perplexity.ai): answers with the sources it used. Open the sources before you trust it.
**NotebookLM** (notebooklm.google.com): turns your notes and PDFs into a study guide, a quiz and a podcast. Needs a Google account.

---

I want to get one thing done today with a free website. Here are five I can use: Photopea (photo editing), TinyWow (PDF and file tools), remove.bg (cut out backgrounds), Perplexity (research with sources) and NotebookLM (turn notes or PDFs into a study guide, quiz or podcast).

1. Ask me what I need to finish today and what files I have (photos, PDFs, notes, a question).
2. Pick the one site that fits best and tell me why in one line.
3. Give me the exact steps on that site, click by click, written for a beginner on a phone.
4. Tell me the free limit or catch I might hit and how to work around it without paying.
5. If none of the five fits, say so plainly instead of forcing one.
